# ASCIIFORGE ETERNAL CONTENT STRATEGY

This document outlines the repeatable weekly rhythm and topic queue to build massive semantic surface area for AsciiForge.

---

## 1. THE REPEATABLE WEEKLY CALENDAR

| Day | Content Type | Count | Focus Themes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monday** | Cluster (Template A) + Micro (Template C) | 1 Pillar + 5 Micro | ASCII Fundamentals (History, character sets, terminal basics) |
| **Tuesday** | Comparison (Template B) | 2-3 Pages | AsciiForge vs Competitors (Patorjk, TextKool, etc.) |
| **Wednesday** | Use-Case (Template E) | 3-5 Pages | Platform Specifics (Discord, GitHub, Social Media) |
| **Thursday** | Deep-Dive (Template F) | 2-3 Pages | Technical Features (Standard, Ghost, Doom fonts) |
| **Friday** | Themes (Template E) | 5-10 Pages | Creative Collections (Animals, Gaming, Memes) |
| **Saturday** | Audit & Interlinking | N/A | Check 404s, internal link health, anchor text optimization |
| **Sunday** | Refresh & Gallery | N/A | Update existing content with 2026 data + new gallery uploads |

## 3. THE ETERNAL WEEKLY RHYTHM (CADENCE)

*   **Weekly Pillar:** 1 per week (3,000+ words)
*   **Weekly Use-Case/Theme Collections:** 2–3 per week
*   **Weekly Font/Technique Deep-Dives:** 2–3 per week
*   **Weekly Comparisons:** 1–2 per week
*   **Daily Clusters:** 3–5 per day
*   **Daily Programmatic (Use-Case/Theme):** 5–10 per day
*   **Daily Micro-Answers:** 5 per day
*   **Weekly indexed pages target:** 50–100 new pages indexed

## 4. PRIORITY TOPIC QUEUE (FIRST 4 WEEKS)

### Week 1: Authority Engine - Fundamentals
*   **Pillar:** "The Ultimate Guide to ASCII Art in 2026"
*   **Comparisons:** AsciiForge vs Patorjk, vs TextKool
*   **Use-Cases:** Discord, GitHub Readmes
*   **Fonts:** Standard Font Deep-Dive, Ghost Font Deep-Dive
*   **Micro:** "What is ASCII Art?", "What is a Figlet Font?"

### Week 2: History & Techniques
*   **Pillar:** "The Complete History of ASCII Art"
*   **Comparisons:** AsciiForge vs Manytools, vs AsciiFlow
*   **Use-Cases:** Social Media Posts, Terminal/CLI Art
*   **Fonts:** Doom Font Deep-Dive, Big Font Deep-Dive
*   **Micro:** "What is a Teleprinter?", "What is BBS Culture?"

### Week 3: Image Conversion & Advanced Topics
*   **Pillar:** "The Complete Guide to Image to ASCII Art"
*   **Comparisons:** AsciiForge vs RapidTables, vs ASCII Art Generator
*   **Use-Cases:** Gaming Profiles, Email Signatures
*   **Fonts:** Banner Font Deep-Dive, Bubble Font Deep-Dive
*   **Micro:** "What is Dithering?", "What is Character Encoding?"

### Week 4: Creative & Themed Collections
*   **Pillar:** "The Ultimate Guide to Creative ASCII Art"
*   **Comparisons:** AsciiForge vs CopyPasteCharacter, vs FSymbOLS
*   **Use-Cases:** Creative Writing, Website Headers
*   **Fonts:** Script Font Deep-Dive, Shadow Font Deep-Dive
*   **Micro:** "What is the Demoscene?", "What is ANSI Art?"
