This document defines the repeatable weekly strategy for building massive semantic surface area for AsciiForge.

---

## 2. THE ETERNAL WEEKLY RHYTHM (Cadence)

*   **Weekly Pillar:** 1 massive guide (3000+ words) per week.
*   **Daily Clusters:** 3–5 educational articles per day.
*   **Daily Programmatic:** 5–10 Use-Case/Theme pages per day.
*   **Daily Micro-Answers:** 5 snippet-targeted pages per day.
*   **Weekly Indexed Target:** 50–100 new high-quality URLs.
