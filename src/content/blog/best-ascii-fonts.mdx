# Best ASCII Fonts for Text

When generating ASCII art from text, the font you choose makes all the difference. While there are hundreds of options (often formatted as `.flf` Figlet fonts), some stand out for their clarity, style, and nostalgia.

Here are a few of the best:

## 1. Standard
The classic. It's clean, readable, and strikes the perfect balance between size and legibility. It's the default for a reason.

## 2. Slant
Dynamic and fast-looking. The slanted characters give your text a sense of motion, making it great for logos or headers.

## 3. Ghost
Soft, eerie, and rounded. Ghost is perfect when you want a distinct, bubbly look that still maintains readability.

## 4. ANSI Shadow
Bold and blocky with a distinct drop shadow effect. This font is visually striking and perfect for terminal welcome screens.

## 5. Doom
Inspired by the classic game, this font is massive, aggressive, and impossible to ignore. Use it when you want your text to truly dominate the screen.
